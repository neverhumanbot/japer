  \colheading{Why Binary $\{0,1\}$ Labels Stall Every Tabular Paradigm}
  {\fontsize{7.95}{9.85}\selectfont
  On pre-incision CABG data, two physiologically identical patients frequently receive opposite binary labels due to unmeasured intraoperative ischemia, cross-clamp variance, or hospital surveillance differences (11.7\%--34.8\% incidence across centers). Consequently, all six modern tabular paradigms trained on raw $\{0,1\}$ at $N=50{,}000$ collapse into a razor-thin \textbf{30.8\,bps} band (0.67115--0.67423).

  \vspace{2.2pt}
  Stage-1 EWMA-hardened gene-locked PSO evolves a continuous consensus target $z^* \in [0,1]^{50\text{k}}$ across whole-hospital shuffled GBDTs and Ridge while pinning the 20\% most confident consensus cases. Independent biological check: among original $y{=}0$ training cases, top-15\% $z^*$ patients exhibit \textbf{3.33\x{}} sepsis, \textbf{2.51\x{}} renal failure, and \textbf{2.33\x{}} mortality on held-out post-op complications never seen during solving.
  }
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{Dual-View PLR-FT \& Zero-NFS Distributed Compute}
  {\fontsize{7.95}{9.85}\selectfont
  \textbf{\wordmark{}} ingests strictly \textbf{207 raw predictors} ($P=215$ encoded) via two fold-internal numerical views --- 512-quantile Gaussianized + winsorized signed-$\log(1+|x|)$ --- embedded through 6-frequency learnable PLR sinusoids, projected into 24 clinical subsystem tokens + \tcli{[CLS]} ($d{=}192$, 3 Pre-Norm GELU layers, 6 heads), and trained with soft BCE + $0.15\x$ pairwise ranking + top-3 SWA. At inference it averages \textbf{20 lightweight passes} with zero Stage-1 feature plumbing.

  \vspace{2.2pt}
  Offline evolution ran on a custom 10-node SSH-piped pull queue (\tcli{scripts/target\_solver/}): 601 cores, \tcli{/dev/shm} \tcli{mmap\_mode='r'}, single-thread BLAS/OMP pinning, \tcli{uint16} genome streaming ($\sim$75\,KB), in-node sufficient-stats reduction ($>50\x$ WAN cut), OS idle wrappers (\tcli{chrt -i 0}), and live load backpressure --- hitting \textbf{135--142 fits/s} (Phase 1) with zero \tcli{y\_test} on workers.
  }
