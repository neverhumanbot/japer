\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{Why Hospital AI Fails Outside Its Home Center}
  {\fontsize{8.35}{10.4}\selectfont
  Postoperative atrial fibrillation (POAF) complicates nearly \textbf{1 in 4} coronary bypass surgeries and is associated with stroke, longer ICU stays, and readmissions. Identifying elevated risk \textbf{before incision} gives teams a way to design and test preventive pathways.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Yet our national audit exposed two costly blind spots: \textbf{(1)~standard calendar-year validation (\texttt{2017}) shares 99.5\% of hospitals} with development, inflating apparent accuracy by \textbf{+2.0 full AUC points}; and \textbf{(2)~on truly unseen hospitals}, every world-leading binary-label model --- including 200M--400M-parameter foundation models from Google, NVIDIA, and PriorLabs --- hits a tight \textbf{0.671--0.674 ceiling}.
  }
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{The Profound Shift: Evolve the Target, Not Just the Model}
  {\fontsize{8.35}{10.4}\selectfont
  Binary surgical labels ($\{0, 1\}$) discard continuous physiological vulnerability hidden beneath unmeasured intraoperative noise. \textbf{\wordmark} flips the paradigm from brute-force model scale to \textbf{supervision quality}: strictly inside a 50,000-patient training cohort (zero test labels ever touched), an evolutionary teacher synthesizes smooth continuous risk targets ($z^* \in [0,1]$).

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Trained on $z^*$ using \textbf{strictly the same 207 standard pre-incision EHR variables}, a compact clinical self-attention transformer reaches \textbf{0.67629 ROC-AUC} across 74 unseen hospitals --- surpassing every individual frontier foundation model and tuned GBDT, improving discrimination in all three bagged foundation models when shared, and recovering \textbf{66.7\% of a 7\x{} data expansion} without extra data buys or heavy foundation-model serving.
  }
\end{minipage}

\briefsection{03}{How to Pilot Safely --- and Honest Governance Boundaries}

\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.478\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{3-Step Clinical Translation Roadmap}
  \vspace{1.5pt}
  {\fontsize{7.85}{9.9}\selectfont
  \stepbadge{1}\;\textbf{Local Shadow Audit (30 Days):} Score your surgical center's recent isolated-CABG records using the frozen 207-variable pre-incision pipeline and assess local calibration and top-decile enrichment.\\[4.5pt]
  \stepbadge{2}\;\textbf{Frozen Prospective Confirmation (STS v4.20+):} Lock the continuous-target recipe, teacher inputs, and transformer weights strictly prior to opening a modern untouched multi-center STS cohort.\\[4.5pt]
  \stepbadge{3}\;\textbf{Targeted Prophylaxis Trial Design:} Pair pre-incision risk enrichment ($\sim$44.9\% top-decile risk) with prospective randomized evaluation of preventive surgical or pharmacologic protocols (e.g., PALACS).}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.494\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \begin{govbox}{Governance, Clinical Safety \& Honest Limits}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em,itemsep=1.7pt,topsep=1pt]
      \item \textbf{Retrospective selection caveat:} Although test labels never touched training workers, 103 arms were evaluated on the 21k holdout; CIs condition on selected runs. Against identical binary FT-Transformer, $+16.0$\,bps ($[-2.1,+35.3]$) spans zero.
      \item \textbf{Evidence required before clinical use:} Confirmatory prospective validation on an unused STS release and randomized interventional testing are required before changing patient care.
      \item \textbf{Hospital heterogeneity \& ascertainment:} STS \tcli{COtAFib} records treatment-requiring AF/flutter (not all silent telemetry episodes), and center rates span \textbf{11.7\%--34.8\%}; new sites need local intercept recalibration.
      \item \textbf{Strict data stewardship:} Record-level STS data stay behind institutional DUA; public artifacts ship aggregate verified receipts only.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{govbox}
\end{minipage}
