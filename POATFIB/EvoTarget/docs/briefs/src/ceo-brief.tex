  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{Why Hospital AI Fails Outside Its Home Center}
  {\fontsize{8.3}{10.3}\selectfont
  Postoperative atrial fibrillation (POAF) complicates nearly \textbf{1 in 4} coronary bypass surgeries --- driving stroke, prolonged ICU stay, readmissions, and billions in preventable surgical cost. Crucially, the highest-leverage preventive interventions (such as posterior pericardiotomy or intensified rhythm protocols) must be decided \textbf{before the first incision}.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Yet our national audit exposed two costly blind spots: \textbf{(1)~standard calendar-year validation (\texttt{2017}) shares 99.5\% of hospitals} with development, inflating apparent accuracy by \textbf{+2.0 full AUC points}; and \textbf{(2)~on truly unseen hospitals}, every world-leading binary-label model --- including 200M--400M-parameter foundation models from Google, NVIDIA, and PriorLabs --- hits a tight \textbf{0.671--0.674 ceiling}.
  }
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{Two Breakthroughs: Evolve Targets \& Evolve Programs}
  {\fontsize{8.3}{10.3}\selectfont
  Binary surgical labels ($\{0, 1\}$) hide continuous physiological risk beneath unmeasured intraoperative noise. \textbf{EvoTarget-FT} evolves smooth continuous targets ($z^*$) strictly inside a 50,000-patient training cohort (verified stable across \textbf{1,000 generations / 256,000 fits} with zero collapse), reaching \textbf{0.67653 ROC-AUC} on strictly 207 raw EHR variables --- beating identical binary FT ($+18.9$\,bps, CI $>0$) and lifting five model families.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  In parallel, fixing six bottlenecks in our GPU-guided genetic programming engine (\textbf{JAIN Ultra GP v2}) leaps \textbf{+39.8\,bps} to \textbf{0.67565}, beating every binary ELO baseline alone. Combining both paradigms (\textbf{Dual-Paradigm Fusion}) reaches \textbf{0.67668 AUC / 0.00724 ECE}, recovering \textbf{79.2\% of a 7\x{} national data expansion}.
  }
\end{minipage}

\briefsection{03}{How to Pilot Safely --- and Honest Governance Boundaries}

\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.478\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{3-Step Clinical Translation Roadmap}
  \vspace{1.5pt}
  {\fontsize{7.8}{9.8}\selectfont
  \stepbadge{1}\;\textbf{Local Shadow Audit (30 Days):} Score your center's recent isolated-CABG records using the frozen 207-variable pre-incision pipeline (20 transformer $+$ 30 lightweight GP passes) with local intercept recalibration.\\[4.2pt]
  \stepbadge{2}\;\textbf{Frozen Prospective Confirmation (STS v4.20+):} Lock target evolution, GP pipelines, and fusion weights strictly before opening an untouched modern multi-center STS cohort.\\[4.2pt]
  \stepbadge{3}\;\textbf{Targeted Prophylaxis Trial Design:} Pair pre-incision top-decile enrichment ($\sim$44.9\% POAF) with prospective randomized testing of surgical or pharmacologic prevention (e.g., PALACS).}

  \begin{govbox}{Governance, Clinical Safety \& Honest Limits}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=1pt]
      \item \textbf{Retrospective selection caveat:} Although test labels never reached training, evolution, or GP workers, 140+ model--target arms were evaluated across studies; cluster CIs condition on selected runs.
      \item \textbf{Nested raw-only power:} Strictly nested 207-raw EvoTarget-FT captures 65.2\% of the gain ($+12.3$\,bps) with $<1$\,bp outer-fold effect, though its standalone CI ($[-3.3, +28.5]$) spans zero at 74 hospitals.
      \item \textbf{Hospital heterogeneity \& ascertainment:} STS \tcli{COtAFib} records treated AF/flutter (rates span \textbf{11.7\%--34.8\%} across centers); new hospitals require lightweight local intercept recalibration.
      \item \textbf{Not yet a bedside device:} Prospective STS v4.20+ validation and randomized trial confirmation are required before changing clinical care.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{govbox}
