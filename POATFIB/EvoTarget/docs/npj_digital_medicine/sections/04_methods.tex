\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Cohort provenance, skip-logic reconstruction, and zero-leakage variable audit}
We analyzed all 368,132 isolated-CABG procedures recorded in STS ACSD version~2.81 (July~1, 2014--June~30, 2017; 1,091 US surgical centers across all nine US Census divisions).\cite{sts2014v281,shahian2018sts,obrien2018sts2} Secondary analysis of de-identified national registry data was performed under institutional Data Use Agreement governance, qualifying for institutional review board exemption, and adheres strictly to TRIPOD+AI and PROBAST+AI standards (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_tripod}).\cite{collins2024tripodai,moons2025probastai}

Three forensic preprocessing steps preceded modeling (full ledger in Supplementary Section~S1). First, under STS Sequence~4930, treatment-requiring postoperative atrial fibrillation or flutter (\cli{COtAFib}) is populated only when the parent complication indicator \cli{Complics == 1}; uncomplicated courses (\cli{Complics == 2}; 232,783 rows, 63.31\%) leave \cli{COtAFib} structurally blank. Filtering on non-missing \cli{COtAFib} therefore induces collider bias.\cite{hernan2020causal} Reconstructing parent--child skip logic recovered \textbf{367,695 labeled cases} (437 indeterminate records excluded) with \textbf{89,337 POAF events (24.30\%)}, corroborated by older patient age (68.5 vs.\ 63.2 years) and higher discharge amiodarone (74.3\% vs.\ 14.7\%) and warfarin (16.5\% vs.\ 1.8\%) utilization. Second, auditing all 638 raw columns individually retained strictly \textbf{207 pre-incision predictors} (206 numeric/ordinal/binary indicators plus 9-level \cli{Region}) while permanently purging seven early-position postoperative or duration leaks (\cli{LOS}, \cli{PLOS}, \cli{ORDuration}, \cli{SIDuration}, \cli{SurgtoMort}, \cli{SurgtoRead}, \cli{SurgtoSternInf}), perfusion/cross-clamp times, discharge medications, STS risk scores, auxiliary complications, and site/surgeon identifiers. Third, non-monotonic sentinel codes (``Not Documented'') were decoded prior to score calculation, and recorded preoperative AF (\cli{ArrhythAFib} codes 2--3; 1,900 cases, 0.52\%) was flagged for sensitivity evaluation ($n=21{,}103$ holdout cases without recorded preoperative AF).

\subsection{Split-geometry audit, unseen-hospital transport, and seen-hospital in-network design}
Five partition geometries were audited across all 367,695 labeled procedures. Holding out calendar year 2017 ($N=61{,}552$) against 2014--2016 ($N=306{,}143$) retains 99.5\% hospital overlap (998 of 1,003 centers seen in training), spans threefold inter-hospital incidence dispersion (12.2\%--34.2\% across 928 centers with $\ge 100$ cases, 5th--95th percentile; overall 11.7\%--34.8\%, SD~6.68--6.97~pp), confounds half-year support (2014 H2 vs.\ 2017 H1), exhibits missingness drift up to $+6.05$~pp (\cli{LVSD}, \cli{LVEDD}, \cli{PctStenAM}; $\mathrm{Max}\,|\mathrm{SMD}| = 0.2793$), and yields adversarial shift AUC of 0.6208 overall (0.5908 hospital-grouped).

To rigorously compare cold-start external generalization with in-network institutional deployment, we constructed two matched partitions of identical natural-prior holdout size ($N=21{,}241$) and identical nested balanced benchmark size ($N=50{,}000$, accommodating frontier tabular foundation model context budgets\cite{jaeger2026tabpfn35,qu2026kumotabular,kong2026tabfm}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Unseen-Hospital External Validation (0.0\% overlap):} Cluster-level multivariate anticlustering\cite{papenberg2021anticlustering} optimizing covariate proxy loss plus $50\times$ 32-D whitened SPlit Energy Distance\cite{joseph2022split,szekely2013energy} (monitoring multi-kernel $\mathrm{MMD}^2$\cite{gretton2012kernel}) partitioned the 1,091 hospitals into \textbf{1,017 development centers} ($N=346{,}454$) and \textbf{74 strictly unseen holdout centers} ($N=21{,}241$, 5,160 events, 24.29\%), reducing Energy Distance 4.1$\times$ ($0.01732 \to 0.00427$) and $\mathrm{Max}\,|\mathrm{SMD}|$ 3.5$\times$ ($0.3572 \to 0.1028$) relative to random hospital grouping. Class-conditional support-point subsampling\cite{joseph2022split} nested an exact 50,000-patient balanced benchmark (25k/25k across five hospital-disjoint 10,000-row CV folds) strictly within the 1,017 development centers.
  \item \textbf{Seen-Hospital In-Network Validation (100.0\% overlap):} Within-hospital outcome-stratified partitioning guaranteed that \emph{all 1,091 US surgical centers} appear in both the full development set ($N=346{,}454$; 1,091/1,091 centers) and the nested balanced 50,000-patient benchmark (1,091/1,091 centers; 5 stratified 10,000-row folds), with zero patient overlap and a matched natural-prior holdout of $N=21{,}241$ patients across 1,086 seen centers (0 unseen hospitals; 24.30\% POAF). Models were evaluated both on strictly 207 patient-only predictors and under in-network site calibration incorporating strictly train-only Empirical-Bayes (EB) shrunk hospital log-odds deviations $\hat{\delta}_h = \operatorname{logit}\!\big((e_h + m_0\pi)/(n_h + m_0)\big) - \operatorname{logit}(\pi)$ computed strictly out-of-fold inside training folds (zero test-label access).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Baseline clinical scores, tabular foundation models, and feature-engineering audit}
Six bedside scores calibrated strictly on the 50k development cohort (Mariscalco POAF,\cite{mariscalco2014poaf} COM-AF,\cite{burgos2021comaf} $\mathrm{CHA_2DS_2\text{-}VASc}$,\cite{lip2010cha2ds2vasc} HATCH,\cite{devos2010hatch} preoperative Mathew proxy,\cite{mathew2004multicenter} and age alone) and six world-leading binary tabular learning families\cite{grinsztajn2022why,erickson2025tabarena}---tuned 5-fold CatBoost,\cite{prokhorenkova2018catboost} LightGBM,\cite{ke2017lightgbm} XGBoost,\cite{chen2016xgboost} PriorLabs TabPFN-3.5 (50k native and $5\times 10\text{k}$ bagged),\cite{hollmann2025tabpfn,jaeger2026tabpfn35} NVIDIA KumoTabular-Large v2 ($5\times 10\text{k}$ with mandatory 256-row train-only class-orientation guard),\cite{qu2026kumotabular} and Google TabFM v1.0.0 ($5\times 10\text{k}$, 80 NNLS estimators)\cite{kong2026tabfm}---were evaluated under a process-wide sealed-label audit hook. Full-registry 346k CatBoost provided the 7$\times$ scaling reference, and 92 deterministic/OOF agent-engineered clinical features (including CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR\cite{inker2021ckdepi} and ALBI grade\cite{johnson2015albi}) were audited independently (Supplementary Section~S2).

\subsection{EvoTarget-FT, JAIN Ultra GP v2, and Dual-Paradigm Fusion}
In Stage~1 of \textbf{EvoTarget-FT}, an evolutionary particle-swarm solver distills\cite{hinton2015distilling} continuous training targets $z^{(g)} \in [0,1]^{50{,}000}$ strictly inside development across 256 whole-hospital shuffled multi-family fits per generation (CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, Ridge) using EWMA hardening $z^{(g+1)}=(1-\beta_g)z^{(g)}+\beta_g\tilde{z}^{(g)}$ ($\beta_1=0.2858 \to \beta_{20}=0.1589$) while gene-locking the 20\% most confident consensus rows. Stability was audited through 1,000 generations (256,000 fits), with Generation~20 ($z^*$) frozen for Stage~2 training of a Dual-View PLR clinical self-attention transformer\cite{gorishniy2021revisiting,gorishniy2022embeddings} (512-quantile Gaussianized and winsorized signed-log sinusoidal embeddings, learned missingness tokens, $T=24$ clinical tokens, $L=3$ Pre-Norm GELU layers, 6 heads, 4 seeds $\times$ 5 folds, top-3 SWA,\cite{izmailov2018swa} composite BCE $+$ pairwise ranking loss) consuming strictly 207 raw predictors. A strictly nested 5-fold outer-loop Gen-20 evolution on strictly 207 raw features (zero validation-hospital access) plus a global 207-raw control formed an exact 2$\times$2 causal decomposition.

For \textbf{JAIN Ultra GP v2}, profiling legacy JAIN revealed six bottlenecks (1-worker search, 1~GiB RAM cap, 20k row cap, IID splits leaking hospital incidence, RMSE fitness, single-model refits). Re-engineering resolved all six via 24 parallel GP workers, 8~GiB RAM, full 50k training, 64-bin Newton soft-BCE boosting (\cli{histgbm2}) with optional binary-$y$ early stopping (\cli{with\_stop\_target}), hospital-grouped CV optimizing binary ROC-AUC, CUDA learned-ranker guidance, and 64-thread fold-bagging across six evolved operator families (\cli{ensemble}, \cli{ridge\_gcv}, \cli{elasticnet}, \cli{histgbm2}, \cli{ridge}, \cli{histgbm}; full DSLs in Supplementary Section~S3). \textbf{Dual-Paradigm Fusion} combines JAIN Ultra GP v2 and EvoTarget-FT via strictly development-OOF logit weighting.

\subsection{Calibration, cluster bootstrap inference, clinical utility, equity, and AI disclosure}
All balanced 50k models were recalibrated to natural clinical prevalence using fold-OOF Platt scaling\cite{platt1999probabilistic} plus the development-only Bayes offset ($\operatorname{logit}(\hat{p}_{\mathrm{nat}}) = \operatorname{logit}(\hat{p}_{\mathrm{bal}}) - 1.136479$ for Regime~1; $-1.136495$ for Regime~2).\cite{king2001logistic,saerens2002adjusting} Calibration was measured via 15-bin ECE,\cite{guo2017calibration,vancalster2019calibration} ICI,\cite{austin2019ici} logistic slope, O:E ratio, and Brier score. All 95\% confidence intervals and paired contrasts used 5,000-draw whole-hospital cluster bootstrapping\cite{davison1997bootstrap,obuchowski1997clustered} (seed~42) across all 74 unseen hospitals (Regime~1) or all 1,086 seen hospitals (Regime~2). Clinical utility was evaluated via Decision Curve Analysis (Net Benefit and unnecessary interventions avoided per 100 patients),\cite{vickers2006decision} fairness across 25 prespecified clinical/demographic subgroups, site transport via 74-hospital funnels and chronological 2014--2016$\to$2017 empirical-Bayes site intercept recalibration, and interpretability via standardized multivariable surrogate regression.
