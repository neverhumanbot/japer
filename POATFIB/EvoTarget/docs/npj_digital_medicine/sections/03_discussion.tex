\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

This national audit of 368,132 isolated-CABG procedures across 1,091 US surgical centers resolves a pervasive paradox in clinical digital medicine: why published POAF prediction models claim AUCs exceeding 0.85 yet degrade to a median external AUC of 0.61 under PROBAST appraisal,\cite{fields2025poaf,lopeztuesta2026poaf} and how zero-leakage auditing, continuous-target learning, parallel genetic programming, and explicit hierarchical site calibration define a rigorous standard for perioperative risk modeling.

Our preprocessing and split-geometry audits prove that dataset engineering exerts as much leverage over reported clinical AI accuracy as model architecture itself. Ignoring STS parent--child skip logic (\cli{Complics == 1}) removes 63.3\% of uncomplicated courses and induces collider bias,\cite{hernan2020causal} positional slicing retains seven hidden postoperative duration leaks, and conventional calendar-2017 holdouts retain 99.5\% hospital overlap while confounding half-year coverage with missingness drift---concealing a $-1.99$-AUC-point external drop when identical models are transported to strictly unseen hospitals. Our controlled 100\% seen-hospital experiment confirms this mechanism directly: merely ensuring all 1,091 hospitals appear in training inflated patient-only AUCs by $+28$ to $+64$ bps even with hospital identifiers withheld, driven by implicit hospital fingerprinting of institutional coding and practice patterns ($r=0.088$--$0.143$ with true site POAF deviation). Frontier tabular foundation models---TabPFN-3.5, KumoTabular-Large v2, and TabFM---gained nearly twice as much from hospital familiarity ($+49$--$64$ bps) as Dual-Paradigm Fusion ($+28$ bps), underscoring that in-context tabular transformers must be benchmarked on strictly hospital-disjoint cohorts before claiming external clinical transportability.

Across strictly unseen hospitals on 207 raw pre-incision variables, all six leading 50,000-patient binary learning families compressed tightly between 0.6712 and 0.6746, reflecting irreducible intraoperative biological stochasticity.\cite{echahidi2008mechanisms,dobrev2019postoperative} Both proposed frameworks broke cleanly above this binary ceiling without leakage: 1,000-generation continuous-target distillation (EvoTarget-FT) converged stably without late-stage collapse and lifted all seven model families---including $+27$ to $+97$-bp gains on zero-shot tabular foundation model regressors---while resolving six engineering bottlenecks in JAIN Ultra GP v2 rescued parallel genetic programming by $+39.8$ bps. Their orthogonal Dual-Paradigm Fusion achieved 0.6767, decisively outperforming every bedside clinical score ($+316$ to $+1{,}029$ bps) and every 50k binary AI baseline ($+20$ to $+55$ bps, plus $+9.9$ bps over a 3-way binary blend), recovering 79.2\% of the 7$\times$ 346k national scaling gap while matching or surpassing 346k CatBoost on Average Precision, ECE, 2017 unseen hospitals, and rare clinical tails.

From a health-system implementation perspective, our dual-regime results establish a practical two-tier architecture for national clinical AI deployment. At newly onboarded centers lacking local historical registry volume (cold-start transport), Dual-Paradigm Fusion delivers the highest discrimination (0.6767), near-ideal calibration (slope~0.983, O:E~0.994, ECE~0.0072), clinically transparent physiological attribution ($R^2 = 0.89$, capturing protective preoperative amiodarone), equitable transport across 24 of 25 demographic and volume strata, and monotonic 7.4-fold risk separation (6.07\% to 44.82\% POAF across deciles). In Decision Curve Analysis across actionable 25\%--35\% thresholds for targeted posterior pericardiotomy (PALACS\cite{gaudino2021palacs}) or pharmacologic prophylaxis, Fusion avoided 20.4 to 33.8 unnecessary interventions per 100 patients, outperforming Mariscalco POAF in 100\% of hospital-cluster bootstrap draws. Conversely, once a hospital accumulates historical cases within a national registry or integrated delivery network (in-network deployment), adding a strictly train-only Empirical-Bayes site intercept lifts every 50k model by $+174$ to $+213$ bps (to 0.696--0.699), pushes full-registry 346k CatBoost past 0.70 to 0.7014, and reduces prospective 2017 center-level rate error by 14\%. Moreover, our finding that lower-entanglement 10k-context foundation models combine slightly more orthogonally with explicit 346k hospital intercepts than higher-entanglement 40k--346k models motivates future mixed-effects neural and symbolic training that explicitly disentangles patient risk from institutional random effects.

Several limitations must be weighed when interpreting these results. Although test labels remained strictly sealed during training and inference, iterative benchmarking across more than 140 experimental arms introduces retrospective selection exposure requiring prospective confirmation of our frozen pipeline on contemporary STS ACSD releases (v4.20+). STS \cli{COtAFib} reflects clinically recognized, treatment-requiring postoperative AF rather than continuous telemetry surveillance,\cite{filardo2017telemetry,perezgrovas2023jaha} and v2.81 lacks raw 12-lead ECG waveforms, echocardiographic left atrial volume indices, and perioperative biomarkers that will be essential for pushing cold-start discrimination beyond 0.70.\cite{han2025aiecg,khalpey2026digital} Additionally, while strictly nested 207-raw EvoTarget-FT verified $+12.3$ bps of zero-overlap denoising (65\% of total gain), its 74-hospital cluster CI included zero ($[-3.3, +28.5]$, $P>0=0.942$), reflecting finite cluster precision across 74 hospitals, and institutional priors require ongoing recalibration surveillance to safeguard against center-level coding or practice drift. Overall, pairing leakage-audited continuous-target learning and parallel genetic programming with transparent cold-start versus in-network institutional calibration establishes an actionable, statistically rigorous blueprint for deploying perioperative cardiovascular AI at national scale.
