# STS POAF manuscript red-team review and cut plan

**Submission status.** The artifacts support a retrospective, hospital-disjoint benchmark and exploratory evidence that an evolved target can improve selected models. The headline point estimates reproduce. The measured effects support an exploratory report. Confirmatory interpretation depends on a frozen comparison evaluated in an unused cohort.

### Feature augmentation and calibration uncertainty

We joined the saved raw and augmented probability predictions to the same 21,241 procedures across 74 hospitals and confirmed the outcome by row ID. We resampled 74 hospitals with replacement in each of 5,000 paired draws (NumPy multinomial seed 42), keeping the saved probabilities fixed. For each arm and resample we recomputed patient-weighted expected calibration error (ECE) using 15 equal-width bins. For each bin we summed predicted minus observed risk across the sampled patients, took its absolute value, added across bins, and divided by the total sampled patients.

| 50k configuration | Raw ECE | Augmented ECE | Augmented minus raw ECE (95% hospital interval) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CatBoost five-fold | 0.007780 | 0.006953 | −0.000826 [−0.005077, 0.002463] |
| TabPFN native 50k | 0.006834 | 0.004884 | −0.001950 [−0.004237, 0.002221] |
| KumoTabular bagged | 0.007015 | 0.006170 | −0.000846 [−0.003394, 0.002523] |
| TabFM bagged | 0.009009 | 0.005238 | −0.003771 [−0.005316, 0.001369] |

All four ECE point estimates are lower, and all four hospital-level intervals span zero. On the same rows, log loss and Brier score improved slightly for CatBoost and TabFM and worsened slightly for TabPFN and KumoTabular. Their paired hospital intervals also include zero.
